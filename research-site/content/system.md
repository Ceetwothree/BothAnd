Research pending. This page will map the pipeline phase by phase:

1. **Prevention / diversion** — keeping people from becoming homeless in the first place
2. **Street outreach** — how people are found, engaged, and assessed
3. **Coordinated Entry** — the intake/prioritization system that's supposed to match people to resources
4. **Interim housing** — shelters, bridge housing, tiny home villages, motel-voucher programs
5. **Permanent housing placement** — Rapid Re-Housing (RRH) vs. Permanent Supportive Housing (PSH), and how someone qualifies for each
6. **Aftercare / retention** — what happens after placement, and why people fall back out

Each phase entry will note the typical timeline, the eligibility gates, and which agencies/NGOs operate it (cross-referenced with <a href="agencies.html">Agencies &amp; NGOs</a>).
